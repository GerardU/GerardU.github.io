Monster Agency · Coming to iOS & Android

Your monster.

Run a demolition agency where the workers are giant monsters. Take a contract, send your monster into a living city and get paid for every brick that comes down.

Topple towers, throw beams and fuel tanks, set the block on fire with Cinder or flood the streets with Leviathan. Three cities, a new Daily Demolition every day, Specialist Contracts and challenges to send to friends. Free to play with advertising and optional in-app purchases.
