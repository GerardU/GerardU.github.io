Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Monster Agency: City Smash (the “game”) is published under the independent developer brand Mosart Games (“we”). This policy explains what information is processed when you play. The game has no account and no sign-in. Contact: mosartgames@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

Your contracts, medals, crew, upgrades, purchases and settings are stored on your device, in the game’s private storage. We do not receive them and we do not run a cloud save. Your operating system may include this data in its own device backups.

Purchases

In-app purchases are optional. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never receive your payment-card details. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, gives your device an anonymous app user ID and receives the purchase record (product, date, transaction identifier, store and platform) so that purchases can be delivered, verified and, for permanent unlocks, restored on your device.

Advertising and your choices

The game shows rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one) and interstitial ads through Google AdMob and its partners. Remove Ads and Agency Edition remove interstitial ads; rewarded ads stay optional. AdMob may process device identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID), IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information to show, measure and protect ads.

On iOS the game asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA and the UK, the game asks for consent through Google’s consent form before it requests ads, and offers privacy options to change that choice later. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not ask for a birth date. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child’s information.

Sharing, retention and your rights

We do not sell your information. Progress stays on your device until you delete the game or its data. Apple, Google, RevenueCat and Google AdMob keep the information they process under their own policies; purchase records may be kept to deliver purchases, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations, and may be processed outside your country.

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to your local data protection authority. We hold no account data that identifies you, so most requests are answered by the device itself: deleting the game removes the progress stored on it. To ask about purchase records or ad data, email mosartgames@gmail.com and say which device and which Apple or Google account you used.
